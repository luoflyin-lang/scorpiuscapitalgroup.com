# Media Kit

This page provides standard language for referencing Scorpius Capital Group LLC.

## Standard Name

- English: Scorpius Capital Group LLC
- Chinese: 心宿资本（美国）集团有限公司

## Standard Description

Scorpius Capital Group LLC is a U.S.-based proprietary capital investment company. It does not raise public money, does not manage client assets, and does not offer advisory or wealth-management services.

## Positioning Line

Managing proprietary capital with a long-term mindset and owning only what we understand well enough to hold through time.

## Use Notes

1. Use the full company name where practical.
2. Do not describe the company as a public fund platform or client asset manager.
3. When citing forecasts, portfolio disclosure, or financial notes, identify them as public website materials.
