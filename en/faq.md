# FAQ

## Does the company accept outside capital?

No. The company invests only its own capital.

## Does it provide advisory or asset management services?

No. This site is not an advisory or client-service portal.

## Is the website investment advice?

No. It is a public company and research archive.

## Does the company publish real-time holdings?

No. Portfolio disclosure is not a real-time feed.

## Why maintain this website?

For company presentation, background verification, and research archiving.

## Are there clients, LPs, or public funds?

No.
