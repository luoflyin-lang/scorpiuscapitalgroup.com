# Disclosure Updates

This page explains how disclosure items on the website are updated and archived.

## Update Cadence

1. Financial statements are archived by year and may include interim public notes.
2. Portfolio disclosure is updated based on research and portfolio changes, not in real time.
3. Annual forecasts are typically published once per year and preserved as dated records.
4. Legal and explanatory pages are updated when wording, structure, or disclosure scope changes.

## Reading Notes

1. Archive pages preserve the context of the original publication date.
2. Explanatory pages reflect the current disclosure framework and may be revised over time.
3. If two pages differ, the more recent and more complete page should control.

## What Is Not Provided

1. No real-time portfolio feed.
2. No account-level statements or transaction-level records.
3. No client reporting, because the company does not manage client assets.
