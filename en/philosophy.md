# Investment Philosophy

This page explains how the company approaches research, portfolio construction, and risk.

## Core Approach

1. Focus on businesses with durable economics, understandable models, and strong capital allocation characteristics.
2. Evaluate holdings over multi-year time horizons rather than reacting to every short-term market move.
3. Balance quality, valuation, and portfolio concentration instead of chasing themes or momentum alone.

## Research Lens

The company generally evaluates assets through:

1. business quality and durability,
2. management and governance,
3. cash flow and balance sheet health, and
4. the margin of safety implied by the price paid.

## Holding Period

1. Core ideas are typically assessed over years, not weeks.
2. Long holding periods do not mean positions are permanent.
3. Volatility by itself is usually not a sufficient reason to exit.

## Risk Discipline

1. No client capital means no redemption-driven pressure.
2. Position sizing and concentration are managed with downside awareness.
3. Valuation, thesis drift, liquidity, and macro exposure are monitored continuously.
