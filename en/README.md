# Scorpius Capital Group LLC

> Proprietary capital investment company

Managing proprietary capital with a long-term mindset and owning only what we understand well enough to hold through time.

## Overview

Scorpius Capital Group LLC is a U.S.-based company that invests exclusively with its own capital.

The company does not raise money from the public, does not manage client assets, and does not provide advisory, brokerage, custody, or fund management services.

## Ownership and Governance

1. The company has a single-shareholder structure.
2. Capital decisions, portfolio construction, and risk ownership remain internally aligned.
3. The absence of external LPs or client mandates allows decisions to be made on a long-duration basis.

## Why The Company Exists

The company exists to provide a durable operating structure for proprietary investing, long-term archiving, and disciplined capital allocation without distribution pressure.

## Founder

The founder is the sole shareholder and final decision-maker, responsible for:

1. capital allocation,
2. risk boundaries,
3. portfolio research,
4. annual forecasts, and
5. disclosure and site updates.

## What This Site Is For

This website serves as a public company profile and archive for:

1. company background,
2. disclosure boundaries,
3. long-term portfolio positioning,
4. annual market outlooks, and
5. financial statement archives.
