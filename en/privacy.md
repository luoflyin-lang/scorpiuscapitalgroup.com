# Privacy Policy

This policy applies to `scorpiuscapitalgroup.com` and its public website content.

## Scope

The site is used for company presentation, research archiving, and public disclosure of company-level information. It is not an account portal and does not provide client onboarding or transaction functionality.

## Information We May Collect

1. Information you send voluntarily by email.
2. Basic server and hosting logs such as access time, page path, device type, browser information, and IP address.
3. Technical diagnostics needed to maintain site availability and security.

## How We Use It

1. To respond to legitimate inquiries.
2. To maintain security, availability, and performance.
3. To preserve necessary operating or compliance records.

## What We Do Not Do

1. We do not sell investment products to the public.
2. We do not sell or rent personal information.
3. We do not use the site as a profiling-based marketing funnel.
